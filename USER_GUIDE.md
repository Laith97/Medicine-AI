## Getting Started

### Account Types
- **Doctors**: Medical professionals who provide consultations and manage their practice
- **Patients**: Individuals seeking medical advice and booking appointments
- **Administrators**: Platform managers who oversee system operations

### Accessing the Platform
1. Visit the Medicine-AI website
2. Click **Login** in the top navigation
3. Enter your credentials or register for a new account

## For Doctors

### Dashboard Overview
After logging in, you'll see your doctor dashboard with:
- Today's appointments
- Pending appointment requests
- Recent patient reviews
- Monthly revenue statistics
- Quick action buttons

### Managing Your Profile
1. Go to **More** → **Settings** or click your profile
2. Update your:
   - Personal information
   - Medical specialty
   - Contact details
   - Profile photo
   - Bio and qualifications

### Appointment Management

#### Setting Your Availability
1. Navigate to **More** → **Availability**
2. Click **Create New Slot**
3. Set:
   - Days of the week
   - Time slots
   - Duration per appointment
   - Maximum bookings per slot
4. Click **Save**

#### Managing Appointments
1. Go to **More** → **Appointments**
2. View appointments by status:
   - **Pending**: Require your approval
   - **Confirmed**: Approved appointments
   - **Completed**: Finished consultations
3. Actions available:
   - **Confirm** pending appointments
   - **Cancel** if necessary
   - **Mark as Complete** after consultation
   - **Mark No-Show** for missed appointments

### AI Diagnosis Tool
1. Click **AI Diagnosis** in the main navigation
2. Enter patient symptoms and medical history
3. Get AI-powered diagnostic suggestions
4. Use insights to assist your medical decision-making
5. Save cases for future reference

### Patient Cases
1. Go to **Patient Cases** to view all consultations
2. Search and filter by:
   - Patient name
   - Date range
   - Diagnosis
   - Treatment status
3. Click on any case to view detailed medical history

### Blog Management
Create and manage health articles for your patients:

#### Creating Blog Posts
1. Go to **More** → **Blog Posts**
2. Click **New Blog Post**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Engaging headline
   - **Short Description**: Brief summary (max 500 characters)
   - **Content**: Full article with rich text editor
   - **Featured Image**: Upload relevant image
   - **SEO Settings**: Optimize for search engines
4. Choose to **Publish immediately** or save as draft
5. Click **Create Post**

#### Managing Blog Posts
- **View**: Preview your posts
- **Edit**: Update content anytime
- **Publish/Unpublish**: Control visibility
- **Delete**: Remove posts permanently
- Published posts automatically appear on your landing page

### Landing Page Management
Create a professional online presence:

1. Go to **More** → **Landing Page**
2. Customize:
   - **Template**: Choose design style
   - **Colors**: Match your brand
   - **Hero Section**: Main banner with your photo
   - **About Section**: Your background and expertise
   - **Services**: What you offer
   - **Contact Information**: How patients can reach you
3. **Preview** changes before publishing
4. **Publish** to make your page live at `/doctor/your-username`

### Reviews and Testimonials
1. Go to **More** → **Reviews** to see patient feedback
2. View ratings and comments
3. Manage testimonials:
   - Make reviews public on your landing page
   - Add case study details
   - Respond to patient feedback

### Chat with Patients
1. Access **Chat** to communicate with patients
2. View active conversations
3. Respond to patient inquiries
4. Mark conversations as read

### Analytics
Monitor your practice performance:
1. Go to **More** → **Analytics**
2. View metrics:
   - Landing page visits
   - Appointment bookings
   - Patient engagement
   - Revenue trends

### Billing and Invoices
1. Go to **More** → **Billing & Invoices**
2. View:
   - Monthly invoices
   - Payment history
   - Outstanding balances
3. Download invoice PDFs
4. Update payment methods

## For Patients

### Finding Doctors
1. Click **Find Doctors** in the navigation
2. Search by:
   - Specialty (cardiology, dermatology, etc.)
   - Location
   - Availability
   - Ratings
3. View doctor profiles with:
   - Qualifications and experience
   - Patient reviews
   - Available time slots
   - Consultation fees

### Booking Appointments

#### Online Booking
1. Select a doctor from search results
2. Click **Book Appointment**
3. Choose:
   - Preferred date
   - Available time slot
   - Appointment type (consultation, follow-up, urgent)
4. Fill in:
   - Personal details
   - Reason for visit
   - Medical history (if relevant)
5. Submit booking request

#### Guest Booking (No Account Required)
1. Provide your contact information
2. Receive confirmation email
3. Use booking reference to:
   - View appointment details
   - Cancel if needed
   - Leave reviews after consultation

### Managing Your Appointments
1. Go to **My Appointments**
2. View upcoming and past appointments
3. Actions available:
   - **Reschedule**: Change date/time
   - **Cancel**: Cancel appointment
   - **Join Video Call**: For virtual consultations
   - **Download Receipt**: Get appointment confirmation

### Leaving Reviews
After your appointment:
1. You'll receive an email invitation to review
2. Rate your experience (1-5 stars)
3. Write detailed feedback
4. Choose to remain anonymous or public
5. Submit review to help other patients

### Reading Health Articles
1. Visit doctor landing pages
2. Browse **Health Tips & Articles** section
3. Read expert medical advice
4. Share articles on social media

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems
- **Forgot Password**: Use "Reset Password" link
- **Account Locked**: Contact administrator
- **Wrong Credentials**: Double-check email and password

#### Appointment Issues
- **Can't Book**: Check doctor's availability settings
- **Booking Disappeared**: Check spam folder for confirmation
- **Need to Cancel**: Use booking reference or login to account

#### Payment Problems
- **Invoice Not Received**: Check spam folder
- **Payment Failed**: Verify card details and try again
- **Billing Questions**: Contact support through platform

#### Technical Issues
- **Page Not Loading**: Clear browser cache and cookies
- **Features Not Working**: Try different browser
- **Mobile Issues**: Update your mobile browser

### Getting Help
1. **Contact Form**: Use the contact page for general inquiries
2. **Email Support**: info@medcuraai.com
3. **Live Chat**: Available on doctor landing pages
4. **FAQ Section**: Check common questions (if available)

### Browser Compatibility
- **Recommended**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
- **Mobile**: iOS Safari, Android Chrome
- **JavaScript**: Must be enabled
- **Cookies**: Required for login and functionality
